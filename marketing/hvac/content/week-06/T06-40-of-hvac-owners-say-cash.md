# T06 — 40% of HVAC Owners Say Cash Flow Is Their #1 Problem. What's Yours?
**Post date:** Tue 2026-10-20 · **Pillar:** Reviews, marketing & seasonality · **Format:** FB/IG text post

## Full post text

40% of HVAC owners say cash flow is their #1 problem.

Not hiring. Not the big guys buying up the market. Not Google ads. Cash flow.

And when you dig in, it's almost always the same shape:

→ July: slammed. Phone won't stop. Turning work away.
→ October: crickets. Same trucks, same payroll, 30–40% fewer calls.
→ February: praying for a heat wave. (In South Florida, praying for a cold snap.)

The feast pays for the famine — barely. And every year the famine part feels like a surprise, even though it lands on the same dates.

Meanwhile, the shops that stopped riding the rollercoaster all did some version of the same three things:

1. Maintenance agreements — recurring revenue that shows up in slow months on purpose.
2. Working their OWN customer list every shoulder season instead of buying strangers from Angi.
3. Following up on the estimate pile from busy season (that's found money, not new money).

None of that needs more trucks. It needs follow-through — which is exactly the part that's automatable now.

So, honest question: what's YOUR #1 problem right now — cash flow, hiring, phones, or something else? I read every answer, and it genuinely shapes what I post next.
