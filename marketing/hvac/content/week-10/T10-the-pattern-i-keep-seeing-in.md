# T10 — The pattern I keep seeing in every shop I talk to
**Post date:** 2026-11-17 (Tue) · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype / proof · **Format:** Text post (FB/IG)

## Full post text

I've spent the last three months talking to HVAC owners — comment sections, DMs, phone calls, and the first shops I've audited. Small sample, I'll be straight about that. But the same pattern shows up so consistently it's almost spooky.

It goes like this:

The owner thinks the problem is LEADS. "We need more calls."

Then we pull the numbers, and the calls were already there. They just died on the way to becoming money.

A quarter of inbound calls ringing out to voicemail. A stack of estimates from the last 90 days — often $40K–$80K worth at a 5-truck shop — that got quoted once and never touched again. Close rates sitting at 25–30% when followed-up quotes close at roughly double. Review count stuck at 38 while the shop across town sits at 400 and eats the Map Pack.

Not one of these shops had a lead problem. Every one had a leak problem.

And here's the part that gets me: the owners already knew. Not the exact numbers — but ask "what happens to a call at 7pm Friday?" and there's always a pause, and then something like "yeah… we should probably deal with that."

You don't need a guru. You need the pause turned into a number, and the number turned into a fix.

Pull your last 90 days of estimates this week. Add up the ones nobody ever called back. Post the number below if you're brave — or just sit with it. That number is where 2027 starts.
