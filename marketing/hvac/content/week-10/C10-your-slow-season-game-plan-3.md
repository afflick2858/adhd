# C10 — Your slow-season game plan: 3 automations to install now
**Post date:** 2026-11-19 (Thu) · **Pillar:** Reviews, marketing & seasonality (fix-first) · **Format:** Carousel, 7 slides

*Design (Canva): dark green/cream "playbook" look. Number each automation big (1/2/3). Include cost + setup time on every fix slide — specificity is the trust play.*

## Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 (HOOK)
**Headline:** Calls are down 30–40% right now. Good.
**Body:** That's your setup window. 3 automations to install before the January heat calls and the June wave.
**Visual note:** Snowflake/thermometer split graphic. "The Slow-Season Game Plan" as series title.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** 1. Missed-call text-back
**Body:** Rings out → customer gets a text in 10 seconds: "Emergency, or call you right back?" Keeps them off Google.
**Cost:** usually <$100/mo · **Setup:** ~15 min (check your current phone system first — it may be built in)
**Why now:** test it on low volume, mistakes are cheap in November.
**Visual note:** Chat bubble mock-up.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** 2. Estimate follow-up sequence
**Body:** Every open quote gets a text on day 1, 3, 7, and 14 — automatically. Most shops follow up once or never; followed-up quotes close at roughly DOUBLE the rate.
**Cost:** $50–$300/mo depending on your software · **Setup:** an afternoon
**Why now:** your 90-day dead-quote pile is sitting right there to test on.
**Visual note:** 4 checkboxes: Day 1 · 3 · 7 · 14.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** 3. Review request on job close
**Body:** Tech closes the ticket → customer gets a review link automatically. No more "forgetting to ask." 91% of homeowners check reviews; 56% start on Google.
**Cost:** often free inside your field software · **Setup:** ~30 min
**Why now:** every winter job banks reviews for summer's Map Pack fight.
**Visual note:** 5 stars filling in.

### Slide 5
**Headline:** The order matters.
**Body:** Text-back first (fastest win). Reviews second (compounds daily). Follow-up third (biggest dollars, most setup). All three together: typically under $400/mo — less than one recovered service call.
**Visual note:** Simple 1→2→3 ladder graphic.

### Slide 6
**Headline:** What NOT to do in November.
**Body:** Don't buy a $1,000/mo "all-in-one AI platform" because a rep called during your slow season. Install the three basics, run them 60 days, look at the numbers in January. Then decide.
**Visual note:** Red X over "ALL-IN-ONE™" badge.
