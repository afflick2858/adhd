## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~108 words)
85 percent of people who hit your voicemail hang up and call your competitor. Eighty-five percent. Think about what your voicemail actually says. "Thanks for calling. Leave a message." To a homeowner with no AC in Florida, that message means one thing: this company can't help me right now. So she doesn't leave a message. She scrolls back to Google and taps the next number. Here's the math. Say 10 after-hours calls a week hit voicemail. Even if only 3 of those would've booked, at 500 a job, that's 1,500 a week. Six grand a month. Gone quietly. Your voicemail isn't taking messages. It's forwarding customers. Save this — and go listen to your own greeting.
