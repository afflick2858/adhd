# T01 — Why I Only Work With HVAC Companies (Intro Post)
**Post date:** Tue 2026-09-15 · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype · **Format:** FB/IG text post

## Full post text

I'm not an HVAC guy. Let's get that out of the way first.

I've never brazed a line set. I've never crawled an attic in August. You'd laugh at me on a roof.

Here's what I actually am: I'm Ann. I train businesses on AI for a living. And last year I made a decision — instead of helping a little bit of everybody, I'd pick ONE industry and learn it cold.

I picked HVAC. Here in South Florida, where AC isn't a luxury, it's life support.

Why HVAC? Because the numbers shocked me.

Home-service businesses miss about 27% of their inbound calls. Each missed HVAC call is worth $500–$1,200. And 85% of callers who hit voicemail don't leave a message — they just call the next company on Google.

That means the average shop is quietly handing thousands a month to the competitor down the street. Not because the work is bad. Because the phone rang while everyone was on a roof.

You're great at HVAC. That's not the problem. The problem is the office side — calls, quotes, follow-up, reviews — was designed for a world where someone could always pick up the phone.

So here's what I'm doing on this page: naming those leaks, one at a time, with real dollar math. No hype. No "AI will replace everyone" garbage. I'm not selling software. I find where money leaks out of good shops, and I show what actually plugs it.

If you run 2–10 trucks, stick around. This page is for you.

Question for the owners here: what's the one office task you'd pay real money to never touch again?
