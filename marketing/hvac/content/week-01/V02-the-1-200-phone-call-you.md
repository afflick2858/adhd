Last night, around 9pm, your phone rang. That call was worth up to 1,200 dollars. Here's how it went. A homeowner's AC quit. House is 85 degrees, kids can't sleep. She Googles "AC repair near me" and calls the first shop. You. It rings. And rings. Goes to voicemail. Does she leave a message? No. 85 percent of callers don't. She calls the next name on the list. They answer. They get the no-cool call, the repair, maybe the replacement — 500 to 1,200 bucks, sometimes a lot more. You never even knew she called. That's not a one-time thing. That's most nights. This week, check your call log after 6pm. Count them.
