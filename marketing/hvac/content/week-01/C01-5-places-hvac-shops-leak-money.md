# C01 — 5 Places HVAC Shops Leak Money (Scorecard Carousel)
**Post date:** Thu 2026-09-17 · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype (all 5 Leaks) · **Format:** 7-slide carousel

## Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 (HOOK)
**Headline:** 5 places your HVAC shop is leaking money
**Body:** Score yourself as you swipe. Most shops are leaking 3 of the 5.
**Visual note:** Huge bold headline on dark navy background. A dripping-faucet or dollar-drain icon. Nothing else.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** LEAK 1 — Missed calls
**Body:** Home-service shops miss about 27% of inbound calls. Each one: $500–$1,200. Leaking if: after-hours calls go to voicemail.
**Visual note:** Phone icon with a red missed-call badge. "27%" as the biggest element on the slide.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** LEAK 2 — Dead estimates
**Body:** Typical shops close 23–30% of quotes. The other 70% walk — and most never get a single follow-up. Leaking if: you don't know your open-estimate count right now.
**Visual note:** Clipboard/quote icon with "70% walk" stamped across it in red.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** LEAK 3 — Thin reviews
**Body:** 91% of homeowners check reviews before choosing an HVAC contractor. 40 reviews vs. a competitor's 400 = invisible on the map. Leaking if: your techs "forget" to ask.
**Visual note:** 5-star row with only 2 stars filled. Big "91%".

### Slide 5
**Headline:** LEAK 4 — Office admin
**Body:** You (or your spouse) doing invoices, scheduling, and paperwork at 9pm. That's owner hours spent on $15/hr work. Leaking if: the same job gets typed into 3 different systems.
**Visual note:** Clock at 9pm + laptop icon. Muted, tired color tone on this slide.

### Slide 6
**Headline:** LEAK 5 — Feast or famine
**Body:** Shoulder-season calls drop 30–40%. Overhead doesn't. 40% of HVAC owners say cash flow is problem #1. Leaking if: your only slow-season plan is "hope."
**Visual note:** Simple line graph plunging in spring/fall. "40%" highlighted.

### Slide 7 (CLOSE)
**Headline:** Scored 3 or more? You're normal. And leaking.
**Body:** I'm breaking down every leak — with dollar math — over the next few weeks. Follow so you don't miss your leak. Save this as your checklist.
**Visual note:** Recap: 5 leak icons in a row with checkboxes. Follow + save icons.

## Caption
Quick self-check for owners: swipe through and count how many of these 5 leaks your shop has. Missed calls (27% average), dead estimates (70% of quotes walk), thin reviews (91% of homeowners check), late-night admin, and the shoulder-season cash crunch (40% of owners call cash flow their #1 problem).

Most shops I look at score 3 out of 5. That's thousands a month — going to the shop down the street.

Comment your score. No judgment — the point is knowing where the leak is.
