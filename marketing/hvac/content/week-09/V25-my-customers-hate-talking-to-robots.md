## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~110 words)
"My customers hate talking to robots." You know what? You're half right.

Some customers do prefer a human. Especially your maintenance-agreement folks who've known your CSR for ten years. Nobody should take that away from them.

But here's the test. It's 9pm. A homeowner with no AC calls you. Nobody picks up. What do they hate more — a polite AI that answers on the second ring and books a tech for 8am… or your voicemail?

Because 85 out of 100 callers won't leave that voicemail. They call the next company on Google.

Nobody hates robots more than they hate silence. That's the whole argument.
