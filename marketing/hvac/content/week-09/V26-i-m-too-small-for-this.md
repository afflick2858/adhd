"I run two trucks. I'm too small for this AI stuff."

Let's do two-truck math. Say you miss just eight calls a week — that's one a day plus weekends. Industry average says a quarter or more of inbound calls go unanswered, so eight is being kind. If three of those would've booked at $450 to $600 a ticket, that's fourteen to twenty grand a year walking to a competitor. From ONE leak.

A missed-call text-back costs less per month than one service call.

Small shop doesn't mean small leak. It means nobody's watching the bucket.
