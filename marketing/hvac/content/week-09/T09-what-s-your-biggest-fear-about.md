**Post date:** 2026-11-10 (Tue) · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype (objection week) · **Format:** Text post (FB/IG)

## Full post text

Honest question for HVAC owners. No pitch in this one.

What's your biggest fear about putting AI anywhere near your business?

I've been talking to shop owners for three months now, and the same worries come up over and over:

"It'll say something stupid to a customer and embarrass us."

"It'll book a tune-up on top of a no-cool call and my dispatcher will spend Monday untangling it."

"I'm paying $200 a month for another tool nobody at the shop actually uses."

"My CSR will think I'm replacing her."

Here's the thing — every single one of those has happened to somebody. Bad AI setups are real. I've seen a bot quote a flat rate the shop didn't offer. That's not fear-mongering, that's a vendor who set it up in an afternoon and never listened to the call recordings.

The fears aren't dumb. They're the exact right questions to ask BEFORE you sign up for anything.

So let's hear it. What would stop you — or what already burned you? Drop it below. I read and answer every comment, and next Thursday I'm putting the five biggest ones (with straight answers, including where the skeptics are right) into one post.

No links. Just want the real answers.
