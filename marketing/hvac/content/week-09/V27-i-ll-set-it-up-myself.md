## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~120 words)
"I'll just set it up myself." Honestly? You can. You rebuilt a compressor — you can figure out software.

Here's the real parts list. Pick the right tool out of thirty that all promise "never miss a call." Write the call scripts — no-cool versus tune-up versus "where's my tech." Wire it into your scheduler so it books real slots, not doubles. Test it twenty ways a stressed homeowner actually talks. Then listen to call recordings every week, because it drifts.

That's ten to twenty hours up front and a couple hours a month forever. If your evenings are free, do it — I'll cheer you on.

But you'd never let a homeowner install his own condenser and call it a tune-up. Same logic.
