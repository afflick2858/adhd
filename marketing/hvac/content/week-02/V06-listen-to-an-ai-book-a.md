**Post date:** Fri 2026-09-25 · **Pillar:** Missed calls / after-hours + Trust ("show, don't tell") · **Length:** 45s · **Format:** Screen/phone-recording demo

## Hook (0–2s, spoken + on-screen)
"This is a real AI answering an 11pm no-cool call. Just listen."

## How to produce this demo (important)
Record a real call to any AI receptionist demo line — several vendors run public demo numbers (e.g., Rosie, Jobber's AI Receptionist demo, Sameday, Dialzara — check each vendor's site for the current public demo number, or spin up a free trial and call your own test line). Screen-record your phone (iPhone: Control Center screen record with mic ON; speakerphone) so viewers see the call screen + hear both sides. Play the homeowner using the script below. Do NOT fake the AI's audio — the whole point is that it's a real recording. If the vendor's AI phrases things differently, keep THEIR words; the sample dialogue below is what you're aiming to capture, not a voiceover to fabricate.

## Sample call dialogue (what you're trying to capture — homeowner lines are yours)
- **AI:** "Thanks for calling [Demo Company] Air Conditioning, this is the after-hours assistant. How can I help you tonight?"
- **You (homeowner):** "Hi, um, my AC just stopped blowing cold. It's 11 o'clock and it's already 82 in the house."
- **AI:** "I'm sorry — no cold air on a night like this is miserable. I can get someone scheduled. Can I get your name and the address of the property?"
- **You:** "Maria. 4-1-2 Palmetto Drive in Coral Springs."
- **AI:** "Got it, Maria. Is the outdoor unit running at all, or is the system completely off?"
- **You:** "The fan outside is running but it's blowing warm air inside."
- **AI:** "Thanks, that helps the technician come prepared. We have an opening tomorrow between 8 and 10am, or I can flag this for the on-call technician tonight. Which works better?"
- **You:** "Tomorrow morning's fine."
- **AI:** "You're booked for tomorrow, 8 to 10am. I'm texting you a confirmation now. Try to rest — help's on the way."
