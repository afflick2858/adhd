# C02 — What an AI Receptionist Actually Does (and Doesn't)

## Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 (HOOK)
**Headline:** What an AI receptionist ACTUALLY does
**Body:** (and what the hype guys won't tell you it doesn't)
**Visual note:** Split slide — green check left, red X right. Bold headline. No logo clutter.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** ✅ DOES: Answer every call. Every time.
**Body:** 2am no-cool call, Sunday, Christmas, all 5 lines at once. Home-service shops miss ~27% of calls. An AI receptionist misses zero.
**Visual note:** Phone icon with "24/7" badge. "27% ➜ 0%" as the big element.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** ✅ DOES: Ask the right questions
**Body:** Name, address, what's the system doing, is anyone in the house without AC. It follows YOUR intake script — and never forgets a question at 11pm.
**Visual note:** Checklist graphic: name / address / issue / urgency, all checked.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** ✅ DOES: Book the job and text you
**Body:** Puts the call on the schedule, texts the customer a confirmation, sends you or your CSR a summary. Caller never hits voicemail — the 85% who'd hang up stay yours.
**Visual note:** Calendar icon + text-message bubble mockup ("Booked: no-cool, Tues 8am").

### Slide 5
**Headline:** ❌ DOESN'T: Diagnose a system
**Body:** It's not a tech and shouldn't play one. Anything technical gets routed to a human — your on-call tech or a morning callback, your rules.
**Visual note:** Red X over a gauge/tools icon. Keep it honest and plain.

### Slide 6
**Headline:** ❌ DOESN'T: Replace your CSR
**Body:** It catches what she physically can't: after-hours, overflow, both-lines-busy. Your CSR keeps the daytime relationships — and stops getting calls at dinner.
**Visual note:** Two figures side by side: "CSR: daytime + relationships" / "AI: nights, weekends, overflow."

### Slide 7 (CLOSE)
**Headline:** It's not magic. It's a net under the phone.
**Body:** At $500–$1,200 per missed call, the question isn't "is AI cool." It's "how many calls fell through last month?" Tomorrow I'm posting a real recording of one booking a no-cool call at 11pm. Follow so you hear it.
**Visual note:** Safety-net graphic under a falling phone icon. Follow prompt.

## Caption
There's a lot of hype about AI receptionists right now, so here's the honest version — what they actually do (answer 24/7, follow your intake script, book jobs, text summaries) and what they don't (diagnose systems, replace your CSR).

The real story is simple: shops miss about 27% of calls, each worth $500–$1,200, and 85% of voicemail-hitters never call back. The AI's job is just to catch what falls.
