Ten missed calls a week is 6,500 dollars a month. Let me show you the math — grab your own numbers and follow along. Home-service shops miss around 27 percent of their calls. On a roof, in an attic, after hours, CSR already on the line. For a 3-to-5 truck shop, that's easily 10 missed calls a week. Now — not every missed call was a job. Be conservative. Say only 30 percent would've booked. That's 3 jobs a week. Average residential ticket: about 500. Three jobs, times 500, times 4.3 weeks — 6,500 a month.
