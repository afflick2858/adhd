# T02 — What Happens to a Call at Your Shop at 7pm on a Friday?
**Post date:** Tue 2026-09-22 · **Pillar:** Missed calls / after-hours · **Format:** FB/IG text post

## Full post text

Honest question for the owners here.

It's 7pm on a Friday. A homeowner's AC just died. She calls your shop.

What actually happens?

A) You answer it yourself — because the phone is your leash and it has been for 15 years.

B) It rings your CSR's cell. Maybe she picks up. It's Friday night. She has a life.

C) An answering service picks up, mangles the details, and you get a sticky note Monday.

D) Voicemail.

E) Honestly? You're not 100% sure.

No wrong answers — but here's why it matters. About 27% of calls to home-service businesses go unanswered, and 85% of the people who hit voicemail never call back. They just book the next shop on Google. At $500–$1,200 a call, the Friday-night answer to this question is worth real money.

Most owners I talk to answer A. Which works — until you're at your kid's game, or on vacation, or just done for the week. The business shouldn't need your pocket to survive a Friday night.
