# T11 — Thanksgiving week: what the trades taught me about earning trust
**Post date:** 2026-11-24 (Tue) · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype (community/warmth) · **Format:** Text post (FB/IG)

## Full post text

Thanksgiving week thought, no numbers today. (Okay, one number.)

Three months ago I started showing up in HVAC comment sections as an outsider with an opinion about your phones. I expected to get torched. Some days I did — and honestly, some of those comments made my work better.

Here's what this industry taught me about trust:

You don't get it by talking. You get it by showing up in an attic in August. By answering the 11pm no-cool call from the lady whose husband just got out of the hospital. By fixing the thing the last guy — a parts changer — misdiagnosed, and charging fair for it.

Roughly 120,000 HVAC businesses in this country, most of them family shops, most built by someone who started on the tools. You keep strangers' kids cool in summer and warm in winter, and most of your customers never learn your name.

That's the standard I'm trying to hold my own work to. Show up. Do the unglamorous thing. Let the results talk. I've got a long way to go and a small track record — but you all set the bar, and I'm grateful for every owner who's commented, argued, DMed, or let me look under the hood of their business this fall.

Happy Thanksgiving to everyone reading this from a truck, a supply-house line, or an office that smells like burnt coffee and copier toner.

Who taught YOU the trade — or the business side of it? Give them a shout-out below. Let's fill this thread with the names that never make the ads.
