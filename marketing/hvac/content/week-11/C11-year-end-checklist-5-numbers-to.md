# C11 — Year-end checklist: 5 numbers to pull before January
**Post date:** 2026-11-26 (Thu, Thanksgiving Day) · **Pillar:** Reviews, marketing & seasonality (practical, warm) · **Format:** Carousel, 7 slides

*Design (Canva): clipboard/checklist motif, warm autumn palette (this posts ON Thanksgiving — schedule it, expect saves more than comments). Each number slide: the number to pull, where to find it, and "healthy looks like."*

## Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 (HOOK)
**Headline:** Before you plan 2027, pull these 5 numbers.
**Body:** One evening, a notepad, and your own systems. No consultant required. (Save this for the weekend.)
**Visual note:** Clipboard with 5 empty checkboxes.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** ① Calls: rings vs. answers
**Body:** Pull your phone system's log for June–August. Total inbound vs. actually answered.
**Healthy looks like:** 90%+ answered. **Industry average:** ~27% missed. Every miss ≈ $500–$1,200.
**Visual note:** Phone icon, two-column tally.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** ② Estimates: sent vs. closed
**Body:** Last 90 days — quotes out the door vs. quotes that became jobs. Then count how many got a second touch.
**Healthy looks like:** 40%+ close rate. **Typical:** 25–30%, one follow-up or none.
**Visual note:** Funnel graphic.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** ③ Reviews: you vs. your top 3
**Body:** Google your main service + city. Write down the review counts of the top 3 shops, then yours.
**Healthy looks like:** you're within shouting distance. 91% of homeowners check reviews; half won't consider a shop under ~20.
**Visual note:** Podium with star counts.

### Slide 5
**Headline:** ④ The list: customers you could text tomorrow
**Body:** How many past customers with a working phone number/email live in your system? How many are on a maintenance agreement?
**Healthy looks like:** club members = 10%+ of the list and growing. **Typical:** big list, near-zero enrolled.
**Visual note:** Database/rolodex icon.

### Slide 6
**Headline:** ⑤ Admin hours: the 9pm tax
**Body:** Honest count — hours/week you (or your spouse) spend on invoicing, dispatch juggling, re-typing jobs between systems.
**Healthy looks like:** under 5. **Typical:** 10+.
**Visual note:** Clock at 9pm, coffee mug.

### Slide 7 (CTA)
**Headline:** These 5 numbers ARE the audit.
**Body:** Pull them yourself and you're 80% of the way there. Want the other 20% — the dollar figure per leak and the fix order? DM "AUDIT" in December. Enjoy the turkey first. 🦃
**Visual note:** ClearFrequency handle, warm sign-off.
