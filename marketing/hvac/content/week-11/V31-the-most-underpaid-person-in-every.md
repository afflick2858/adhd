## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~110 words)
The most underpaid person in every HVAC company isn't on a truck.

She's the one answering three lines at once while entering yesterday's invoices. She knows which customer needs the same tech every time, which streets flood in August, and which supplier actually has the part. Half the time she's married to the owner and hasn't sent an invoice for her own hours in years.

Industry surveys say the same job typed into different systems, the 9pm paperwork, the whiteboard dispatch — that's ten-plus hours a week of pure re-typing at a lot of shops.

This Thanksgiving week, do two things. Tell her thank you, out loud. Then get her some help — the software kind counts.
