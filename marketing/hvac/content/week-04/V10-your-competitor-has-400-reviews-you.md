## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~115 words)
Your competitor has 400 Google reviews. You have 40. That's the whole ballgame — and here's why. 91 percent of homeowners check reviews before picking an HVAC contractor. 56 percent go straight to Google first. 68 percent won't even consider you under 4 stars, and about half skip any business with fewer than 20 reviews. Now here's the unfair part: the 400-review shop probably isn't better than you. Their techs aren't better. Their installs aren't cleaner. They just ASK. Every job, every time, automatically. Your techs do great work and then drive away without asking, because asking feels weird. So Google thinks you barely exist. The work was never the problem. The asking is. More on the fix Wednesday.
