# C04 — Rate Your Own Shop: The 5-Leak Self-Audit

## Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 (HOOK)
**Headline:** Rate your own shop: the 5-Leak self-audit
**Body:** 10 questions. 2 points each. Grab a pen — most shops score under 12.
**Visual note:** Clipboard/scorecard graphic, big "?/20" badge. Bold headline, dark background.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** LEAK 1 — The phones
**Body:** Q1: Does a human (or anything smarter than voicemail) answer your phone at 9pm? (2 pts) · Q2: Do you know your missed-call count from last week? (2 pts) — Why it matters: ~27% of calls go unanswered, $500–$1,200 each, and 85% of voicemail-hitters never call back.
**Visual note:** Phone icon, leak-1 badge. Questions as a checklist with point values.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** LEAK 2 — The estimate pile
**Body:** Q3: Can you name your open-estimate dollar total right now? (2 pts) · Q4: Does every quote get 4+ automatic touches (day 1/3/7/14)? (2 pts) — Why: typical shops close 23–30%; ~70% of quoted work walks in silence.
**Visual note:** Paper-stack icon, leak-2 badge.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** LEAK 3 — Reviews
**Body:** Q5: Does every completed job trigger an automatic review request? (2 pts) · Q6: Are you within 100 reviews of your biggest local competitor? (2 pts) — Why: 91% of homeowners check reviews; half skip businesses under 20.
**Visual note:** Star icon, leak-3 badge.

### Slide 5
**Headline:** LEAK 4 — The office
**Body:** Q7: Does each job get typed into only ONE system? (2 pts) · Q8: Is the owner (or spouse) doing paperwork after 8pm less than once a week? (2 pts) — Why: owner hours on $15/hr admin work is the most expensive labor in the company.
**Visual note:** Clock + laptop icon, leak-4 badge.

### Slide 6
**Headline:** LEAK 5 — Feast or famine
**Body:** Q9: Do past customers hear from you at least quarterly (tune-ups, maintenance agreements)? (2 pts) · Q10: Could you name your cost per booked job by lead source? (2 pts) — Why: shoulder-season calls drop 30–40%; 40% of owners call cash flow their #1 problem.
**Visual note:** Roller-coaster line graph, leak-5 badge.

### Slide 7
**Headline:** Your score
**Body:** 16–20: tight ship — you're the shop others lose to. · 10–14: solid work, 1–2 expensive leaks. · Under 10: you're likely leaking $3K–$10K/month. Not because you're bad at HVAC — because nobody ever built the catch-net.
**Visual note:** Three score bands as a traffic-light table (green/yellow/red).
