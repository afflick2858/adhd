# T04 — I've Watched Good Shops Get Burned by Marketing Agencies (Honest-Broker Post)
**Post date:** Tue 2026-10-06 · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype · **Format:** FB/IG text post

## Full post text

Almost every owner I talk to has the same scar.

Somewhere in the last five years, they paid a marketing agency $1,500–$2,500 a month. For SEO, or ads, or "brand." The reports had lots of charts. The phone didn't ring more. After 8 months they quit, feeling dumb for staying that long.

Then came the lead sellers. $50–$150 per shared lead — the same "lead" sold to four other shops, everyone racing to call first. Owners tell me flat out: "Angi leads are garbage."

So when somebody like me shows up saying the letters "A" and "I," I understand exactly what face you're making. You've seen this movie.

Here's the thing though — the agencies weren't wrong that you're losing money. They were just looking in the wrong place. The money isn't in more traffic. For most 2–10 truck shops, it's in the leads you ALREADY get and lose:

→ ~27% of your calls go unanswered — $500–$1,200 each
→ ~70% of your estimates walk, mostly with zero follow-up
→ 91% of homeowners check reviews, and your count is starving because nobody asks

You don't have a leads problem. You have a leaks problem. Buying more leads while these leaks are open is pouring water into a cracked bucket — which, conveniently, is exactly what lead sellers get paid for you to keep doing.

I'd rather help you patch the bucket. Sometimes that's AI. Sometimes it's a $0 process change. Either way, the math comes first.

What's your agency horror story? I'll go first in the comments — I've heard some incredible ones. Worst "marketing" money you ever spent?
