## Slide-by-slide

**Headline:** Score your shop in 10 questions. Tonight.

### Slide 2 — LEAK 1: The phone
**Q1. What happens to a call at 7pm on a Friday?**
Answered & bookable (live or AI) = 2 · Answering service takes a message = 1 · Voicemail / my cell if I hear it = 0
**Q2. Missed calls — do you know your number?**
I track it monthly = 2 · I could pull it from the phone system = 1 · No idea = 0
**Visual note:** Phone icon. Footer stat: shops average ~27% missed · $500–$1,200 each.

### Slide 3 — LEAK 2: Estimates
**Q3. A quote goes out and the customer goes quiet. What happens on day 3?**
Automatic text/email goes out = 2 · Somebody usually calls, eventually = 1 · Nothing. We move on = 0
**Q4. Do you know your 90-day close rate?**
Yes, roughly, and we track it = 2 · Could figure it out = 1 · No = 0
**Visual note:** Quote/funnel icon. Footer stat: typical close 25–30% · follow-up ≈ doubles it.

### Slide 4 — LEAK 3: Reviews
**Q5. Does every completed job trigger a review request?**
Automatically, every job = 2 · Techs ask when they remember = 1 · We don't really ask = 0
**Q6. Google your service + city. Are you in the top 3 map results?**
Yes = 2 · Page one, not map pack = 1 · Can't find us = 0
**Visual note:** Stars icon. Footer stat: 91% of homeowners check reviews first.

### Slide 5 — LEAK 4: Admin
**Q7. How many times does one job get typed into different systems?**
Once — it flows through = 2 · Twice = 1 · Three or more (or "don't ask") = 0
**Q8. Hours per week you or your spouse spend on paperwork after 6pm?**
Basically zero = 2 · A few = 1 · It's our whole evening routine = 0
**Visual note:** Paperwork icon. Footer stat: typical shop re-types 10+ hrs/week.

### Slide 6 — LEAK 5: The list
**Q9. When did your past customers last hear from you (not an invoice)?**
Scheduled campaigns, at least quarterly = 2 · Once in a while = 1 · Never = 0
**Q10. What % of your customer list is on a maintenance agreement?**
10%+ = 2 · A handful = 1 · What maintenance agreements = 0
**Visual note:** Database icon. Footer stat: calls drop 30–40% in shoulder season — club revenue doesn't.

### Slide 7 — SCORING
**Headline:** Add it up. /20
**16–20: Tight ship.** You're in the top tier. Your leaks are drips — optimize, don't overhaul.
**10–15: Normal — and leaking.** Most 2–10 truck shops land here. Likely leak: $2K–$5K/month. Fix your two 0-scores first.
**0–9: The bucket has holes.** Probably $5K–$10K/month walking out. Good news: your first two fixes are cheap (text-back + review automation, under $200/mo combined).
**Visual note:** Three score bands, gauge graphic.
