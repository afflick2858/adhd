## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~120 words)
The first thing I check when I audit an HVAC company isn't a spreadsheet. It's a phone call.

With the owner's permission, I call their shop after hours, like a homeowner with a dead AC. Then I write down exactly what happens.

Does it ring six times and dump to a full voicemail box? Does an answering service pick up and just… take a message? Does anything text me back?

Then I do it again during lunch hour. And on a Saturday.

That one test tells me more than any dashboard — because at $500 to $1,200 a missed call, your after-hours phone is either an employee or a hole in the bucket.

Here's the uncomfortable part: you can run this test on yourself tonight. Most owners never have.
