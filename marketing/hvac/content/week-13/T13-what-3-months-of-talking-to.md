# T13 — What 3 months of talking to HVAC owners taught me
**Post date:** 2026-12-08 (Tue) · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype (authority recap) · **Format:** Text post (FB/IG)

## Full post text

Three months ago I started posting into HVAC world as an outsider. 65 posts, a few hundred conversations, and my first audits later, here's what I actually learned — the stuff I didn't have on a spreadsheet going in.

1. Nobody has a lead problem. I came in expecting "we need more calls." What I found, over and over: ~27% of the calls they already get ring out, and 70% of the work they already quote walks away untouched. The money isn't missing. It's leaking.

2. The skeptics were right about more than I expected. Bad AI setups are real — bots quoting prices the shop doesn't offer, "all-in-one platforms" at $1,000+/mo built for 30-truck operations sold to 3-truck shops. If you got burned, you weren't behind the times. You were beta-testing someone else's laziness.

3. The office manager is the whole ballgame. Show me who answers the phone and does the invoicing, and I'll tell you the health of the company. Every good automation I've seen made her job better. Every bad one got installed without asking her.

4. Owners already know where the leak is. Ask "what happens to a call at 7pm Friday?" and there's always that pause. The audit doesn't reveal secrets — it puts a dollar number on the thing you've been meaning to fix since 2023.

5. This industry gives trust slowly and completely. Fair. You've been burned by lead sellers, SEO agencies, and coaches. So I'll keep doing it the slow way: real numbers, free playbooks, honest "you don't need this" answers, and a small but growing track record I won't inflate.

That last one is a promise for 2027, too.

If you've been reading along since September — thank you. Genuinely. If you're new: the whole 5-Leak framework is pinned to my profile in one 60-second video, and Thursday I'm posting a DIY mini-audit you can run on your own shop in 10 minutes.

What's the ONE thing you're fixing in your business before summer? Tell me below. I'll answer every comment with a first step.
