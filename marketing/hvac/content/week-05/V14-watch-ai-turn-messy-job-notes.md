- **Voice:** same AI voice as every video. Let the demo breathe — a 1-second silent beat while the output generates reads as authentic.
- **Recording:** capture at 1080p+, zoom/punch-in on the text in CapCut (screen text is unreadable on phones otherwise). Max 3–4 lines visible per zoom.

- **Captions:** auto-captions ON, bold; highlight "TYPED NOTHING EXTRA" and "NOTHING NEW TO BUY".
- **Speed:** time-lapse the generation to keep total under 45s, but show it really generating — don't jump-cut to a finished result. The watching-it-happen is the proof.
