## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~110 words)
Your wife runs the phones, the books, and the schedule. For free. At dinner.

In half the shops I talk to, the office manager is the owner's wife — and honestly? She's the best-run part of the company. She knows every customer, every tech, every open invoice.

But here's what nobody asks: does she WANT the phone going off at 9pm on a Saturday? During her kid's game? On vacation?

This isn't about replacing her. It's about backup. AI takes the after-hours calls, the missed-call texts, the retyping — she keeps the judgment calls, the customers, the real work.

She built this business with you. She deserves an off switch too.
