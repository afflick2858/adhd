# T05 — How Many Times Does the Same Job Get Typed Into Different Systems at Your Shop?
**Post date:** Tue 2026-10-13 · **Pillar:** Admin/office overload · **Format:** FB/IG text post

## Full post text

Honest question for owners: how many times does ONE job get entered at your shop?

Here's the version I keep hearing:

1. Tech writes it on a notepad in the truck.
2. Tech texts a photo of the notepad to the office.
3. CSR types it into the scheduling system.
4. Someone retypes it into the invoice.
5. Someone copies it AGAIN into QuickBooks.

That's five entries for one water-damaged blower motor.

Multiply by 8–10 jobs a day and you've got hours of typing — usually done by you or your office manager at night, after the real work is done.

Owners I talk to put paperwork at 5–10 hours a week. Call it $50–$100/hour of owner time and that's $1,000–$4,000 a month spent being a typist.

You didn't get your EPA cert to do data entry.

So I'm curious — what's your number? How many times does one job get touched before it's invoiced and in the books?
