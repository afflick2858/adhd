# C12 — The 2027 AI stack for a 5-truck shop (vendor-neutral)
**Post date:** 2026-12-03 (Thu) · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype (vendor-neutral authority) · **Format:** Carousel, 8 slides

*Design (Canva): blueprint/spec-sheet aesthetic — this should look like an equipment submittal, not an ad. Every layer gets a price band and a "skip it if" line. NO vendor names — categories only. That's the whole credibility play.*

## Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 (HOOK)
**Headline:** The 2027 AI stack for a 5-truck shop.
**Body:** Vendor-neutral. Price bands included. With "skip it if" warnings — because half of you don't need half of this.
**Visual note:** Blueprint grid background, stack diagram teaser.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** Layer 1 — Missed-call text-back
**Body:** Rings out → instant text to the caller. The floor. Everybody starts here.
**Runs:** <$100/mo · Setup: ~15 min
**Skip it if:** you genuinely answer 95%+ of calls (check the log — almost nobody does).
**Visual note:** Foundation block of a stack diagram.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** Layer 2 — After-hours / overflow AI answering
**Body:** Answers when nobody can, books simple jobs onto the schedule, flags emergencies to on-call.
**Runs:** $100–$300/mo entry tiers ($1,000+/mo enterprise versions exist — a 5-truck shop doesn't need them)
**Skip it if:** you have a live answering service that actually BOOKS jobs (most just take messages).
**Visual note:** Second block.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** Layer 3 — Estimate follow-up sequences
**Body:** Auto text+email on day 1/3/7/14 for every open quote. Follow-up roughly doubles typical 25–30% close rates. Biggest dollars on this list.
**Runs:** $50–$300/mo (often inside software you already own)
**Skip it if:** you quote fewer than ~5 jobs a month.
**Visual note:** Third block, tagged "BIGGEST $."

### Slide 5
**Headline:** Layer 4 — Review engine
**Body:** Auto-request after every closed job + AI-drafted responses. 91% of homeowners check reviews; the Map Pack pays in free calls.
**Runs:** free–$150/mo
**Skip it if:** honestly? Don't skip this one. It compounds.
**Visual note:** Fourth block with stars.

### Slide 6
**Headline:** Layer 5 — List campaigns (the shoulder-season engine)
**Body:** AI-written seasonal offers + maintenance-agreement pushes to your past customers. Fills February.
**Runs:** mostly labor, $0–$100/mo in tools
**Skip it if:** your customer list is under ~100 contacts (fix the list first).
**Visual note:** Top block.

### Slide 7
**Headline:** Whole stack: roughly $250–$800/mo. Not $3,000.
**Body:** That's the honest range for a 5-truck shop in 2027. One recovered $8K–$15K changeout funds the entire year. If a rep quotes you four figures monthly, you're buying their sales team, not your stack.
**Visual note:** Full 5-block stack with total price band.
