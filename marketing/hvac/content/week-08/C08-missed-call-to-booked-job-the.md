**Headline:** MISSED CALL → BOOKED JOB. THE FULL FLOW, STEP BY STEP.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** 9:47 PM — THE CALL
**Body:** Homeowner's AC dies on a hot night. She calls the first shop on Google. At most shops this rings out — and 85% of callers won't leave a voicemail. They dial the next name. $500–$1,200, gone in 20 seconds.
**Visual:** Phone ringing graphic, "9:47 PM" timestamp, red "NO ANSWER" stamp.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** STEP 1 — THE CALL GETS ANSWERED. EVERY TIME.
**Body:** AI picks up on ring two, any hour. Gets the essentials: name, address, "no cool since 6pm," system age. Sounds like a calm dispatcher, not a robot maze. Real emergencies get escalated to your on-call number.
**Visual:** Chat-style transcript snippet: "I can help — is anyone in the home without AC right now?"

### Slide 4
**Headline:** STEP 2 — IT BOOKS THE JOB
**Body:** It reads your real schedule and offers real slots: "First available is 8–10am tomorrow." Books it, confirms by text. No callback lottery in the morning, no lost-to-the-competitor overnight.
**Visual:** Calendar with 8:00 AM slot filling in, green check.

### Slide 5
**Headline:** STEP 2B — AND IF SHE HANGS UP EARLY?
**Body:** Missed-call text-back fires in under a minute: "Sorry we missed you — this is [Shop] Air. Need service tonight? Reply here." Texts get roughly double the response rate of callbacks. The lead stays yours.
**Visual:** SMS bubble mockup with timestamp "9:48 PM."

### Slide 6
**Headline:** STEP 3 — YOUR TEAM WAKES UP TO A BOOKED JOB
**Body:** CSR opens the day to: job booked, notes attached, customer confirmed. Tech rolls at 8am. After the job: invoice drafted from notes, review request sent automatically. One phone call, zero humans interrupted at dinner.
