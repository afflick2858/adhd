# T07 — Unpopular Opinion: Most Shops Don't Need More Software
**Post date:** Tue 2026-10-27 · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype · **Format:** FB/IG text post

## Full post text

Unpopular opinion from someone who sells AI services: most HVAC shops don't need more software.

I mean it. If I audited your shop tomorrow, odds are I'd find:

→ A scheduling system you pay for and half-use
→ A CRM with 800 customers nobody ever contacts
→ Maybe a lead service charging $50–$150 for shared leads that four competitors also bought

Adding another app to that pile fixes nothing. Software you don't use is just another monthly bill — and this industry has been sold enough of those.

What actually moves the needle in a 2–10 truck shop is embarrassingly unsexy:

1. Answer the phone every time it rings — including 7pm Friday. (A quarter or more of calls go unanswered, at $500–$1,200 a call.)
2. Follow up on every estimate more than once. (~70% of quoted work walks, and most of it was never chased.)
3. Talk to your own customer list twice a year.

That's it. That's the list. AI matters only because it does those three things without you hiring anyone or remembering anything.

The order matters too — which leak is bleeding YOUR shop worst is different for every company. That's the whole reason audits exist: so you fix the $6K/month problem before the $500/month one.

What software are you paying for right now that you barely open? Confession thread. I'll go first in the comments.
