## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~115 words)
AI is not replacing your CSR. Anyone who tells you it is, is selling something.

Your CSR calms down a panicked homeowner with water coming through the ceiling. Knows Mrs. Alvarez wants the same tech every time. Smells when a caller's about to be a problem customer. No AI does that.

Here's what AI actually does: it answers the calls she physically can't. The 11pm no-cool. The three calls that hit at once in July. The Saturday she's at her kid's game. It books the simple stuff and hands her the rest — with notes.

Around a quarter of calls go unanswered at most shops. That's not her failing. That's one person versus a phone that never stops.

AI is her backup, not her replacement.
