## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~120 words)
Three AI tools that waste your money. Two that pay for themselves. And no — I don't sell any of them.

Waste number one: AI social media schedulers. Homeowners with a broken AC search Google. They don't scroll your feed.

Waste two: AI chatbots on a website nobody visits. Fix the phone first.

Waste three: the $3,000 "AI marketing platform" — usually cheap software with a markup, locked into someone else's system.

Now the two that earn. One: after-hours call answering with missed-call text-back. Every saved call is worth $500 to $1,200. Two: automated estimate follow-up. Around 70% of quotes walk, mostly because nobody followed up.

Phones and follow-up. Boring wins. Everything else waits.
