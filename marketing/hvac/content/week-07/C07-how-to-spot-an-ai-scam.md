# C07 — How to Spot an AI Scam (Contractor Edition)
**Post date:** Thu 2026-10-29 · **Pillar:** Trust & anti-hype · **Format:** 7-slide carousel (Canva)

## Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 (hook)
**Headline:** HOW TO SPOT AN AI SCAM — CONTRACTOR EDITION
**Body:** You've been burned by SEO guys, lead sellers, and coaches. The AI wave has the same sharks. Here's the field guide.
**Visual:** Bold text, dark background, warning-triangle accent.

### Slide 2
**Headline:** 🚩 1. THEY LEAD WITH "REPLACE YOUR STAFF"
**Body:** Anyone promising to replace your CSR doesn't understand your business. Real automation answers what she can't — the 11pm calls, the overflow — it doesn't fire anyone.
**Visual:** Red flag icon, crossed-out "replace" text.

### Slide 3
**Headline:** 🚩 2. NO DOLLAR MATH, JUST BUZZWORDS
**Body:** "Leverage AI to transform your business" is a tell. A legit operator talks like this: missed calls run $500–$1,200 each, ~70% of quotes walk without follow-up. If they can't put numbers on YOUR shop, walk.
**Visual:** Buzzword soup graphic vs. clean math line.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** 🚩 3. ONE TOOL FIXES EVERYTHING
**Body:** It's usually white-labeled software they rent for under $200/mo and resell at $1,500 setup + $500–$1,500/mo. The tool might be fine. The markup and the lock-in aren't.
**Visual:** Same box relabeled 3 times with rising price tags.

### Slide 5
**Headline:** 🚩 4. LOCKED CONTRACTS, THEIR ACCOUNTS
**Body:** If the phone number, customer list, or review profile lives in THEIR account, you're renting your own business back. 12-month locks on unproven tools = run.
**Visual:** Padlock over a phone/contact icon.

### Slide 6
**Headline:** ✅ WHAT LEGIT LOOKS LIKE
**Body:** • Names the problem in YOUR numbers before selling anything • Shows a live demo, not a slide deck • Month-to-month, you own every account • Fine saying "you don't need this yet" • Vendor-neutral — gets paid for results, not kickbacks.
**Visual:** Green check list, calmer color shift.

### Slide 7 (close)
**Headline:** THE SHARKS COUNT ON YOU NOT KNOWING THE QUESTIONS

**Visual:** ClearFrequency.ai handle + logo.
