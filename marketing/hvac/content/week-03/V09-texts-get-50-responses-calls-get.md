# V09 — Texts Get 50% Responses. Calls Get 25%. You're Still Calling.
**Post date:** Fri 2026-10-02 · **Pillar:** Unsold estimates / follow-up · **Length:** 35s

## Hook (0–2s, spoken + on-screen)

## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~110 words)
Texts get about 50 percent responses. Calls get maybe 25. And your follow-up plan is still "call them back." Think about your own phone. Unknown number rings at 2pm — do you answer? Neither do your customers. But a text? They read it at the red light, at the school pickup, on the couch at 9pm. SMS follow-up on estimates gets 45 to 55 percent response rates. Phone calls, 20 to 30. It's not that people don't want to hear from you. They don't want to TALK to you. Not at 2pm on a Tuesday. So follow up where they actually live. Their texts. Save this for your CSR — she already knows I'm right.
