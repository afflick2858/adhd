### Slide 2
**Headline:** TEXT 1 — Day 1 (same day as the quote)
**Body:** "Hi [Name], it's [You] from [Shop]. Great meeting you today — your quote for the [system] is attached. Any questions at all, just text me back here. No rush."

**Body:** "Hi [Name], quick check-in — did any questions come up about the quote? Happy to walk through financing options too. Most folks are surprised what the monthly comes to."
**Visual note:** Text bubble mockup. Label: OPENS THE MONEY CONVERSATION.

### Slide 4
**Headline:** TEXT 3 — Day 7
**Body:** "Hi [Name], [You] from [Shop]. Totally understand this is a big decision. One thing worth knowing: install slots for the next 2 weeks are filling up. Want me to pencil you in while you decide?"
