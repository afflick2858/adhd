## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~112 words)
You quoted 80 grand last month. You closed 24. Where'd the other 56 go? Here's the ugly industry number: typical residential HVAC shops close 23 to 30 percent of their estimates. That means around 70 percent of everything you quote... walks. And here's the part that should keep you up: most of those homeowners didn't say no. They said "let me think about it." Then life happened. Kids, work, the credit card bill. Nobody from your shop ever called back. Not once. So the quote died in a drawer. Healthy shops close 40 to 60 percent — and the difference is almost never price. It's follow-up. This week is all about that pile. Go count your open estimates tonight.
