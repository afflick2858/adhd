# T03 — Most Shops Follow Up on Estimates Exactly Once. Or Never.
**Post date:** Tue 2026-09-29 · **Pillar:** Unsold estimates / follow-up · **Format:** FB/IG text post

## Full post text

Most HVAC shops follow up on an estimate exactly once. A lot follow up never.

I get why. Nobody got into this trade to make awkward "just checking in" calls. Your techs would rather braze in a crawlspace than dial a homeowner who went quiet. And by Thursday, this week's no-cools have buried last week's quotes anyway.

But here's what the numbers say:

→ Typical HVAC close rates: 23–30%. About 70% of quoted work walks.
→ 80% of deals take 8–12 touches to close. Not one. Not two. Eight to twelve.
→ Most of the "lost" quotes weren't lost to a competitor. They were lost to silence.

Do the math on your own shop. If you quote $60–$80K a month and close a quarter of it, a single follow-up sequence that recovers even 10 more percentage points is worth tens of thousands a year. One vendor's math puts a 10-point close-rate lift around $28K/month for a mid-size shop. Even if your shop's number is a fifth of that, it's the easiest money in your business.

The fix isn't hiring a salesperson. It's making follow-up automatic — so the quote gets touched on day 1, day 3, day 7, and day 14 whether anyone remembers or not. (Thursday I'll post the exact 4-text sequence.)

Question: what's YOUR follow-up routine right now, honestly? Once? Never? "When I remember"? No shame — just curious how deep this one runs.
