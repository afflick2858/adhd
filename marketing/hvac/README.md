# HVAC AI Profit Audit — Marketing Package

Everything for launching ClearFrequency's HVAC vertical: zero ad spend, organic short-form content + direct outreach. Built Sep 2026.

## Start here, in order

1. **`strategy/offer-and-audit-framework.md`** — the AI Profit Audit offer, pricing ladder, and the 5 Leaks framework (also your audit delivery checklist).
2. **`strategy/30-day-first-client-plan.md`** — the week-by-week plan to land client #1 by mid-October (outreach + FB groups + content, with scripts).
3. **`strategy/content-strategy.md`** — platforms, voice rules, weekly rhythm, production workflow.
4. **`calendar/content-calendar.md`** (+ `.csv`) — all 65 pieces, Sep 14 → Dec 11, 2026. Mirrored on Google Calendar: 7 AM ET post events + Sunday 9–11 AM batch-assembly blocks.
5. **`content/week-01/ … week-13/`** — every piece, ready to produce:
   - **V** files = faceless video kits: hook, word-for-word voiceover script, shot-by-shot plan with stock b-roll search terms, on-screen text, CapCut assembly notes, caption, hashtags. ~10–15 min each to assemble in CapCut (free) using its text-to-speech.
   - **C** files = carousel posts: slide-by-slide text + design notes (build in Canva).
   - **T** files = text posts: copy-paste ready.
6. **`research/hvac-market-research.md`** — the sourced market research behind all of it.

## The one-paragraph strategy

HVAC owners (2–10 trucks, 40–60 years old, mostly ex-techs) live on **Facebook**, not TikTok. Content names the five money leaks (missed calls, dead estimates, weak reviews, admin overload, feast-or-famine marketing) with real dollar math, in trade language, with zero hype — month 1 names the problems, month 2 demos the fixes, month 3 converts. Meanwhile the first client comes from the 30-day plan: a 100-shop South Florida hit list, outreach messages built on one observable leak per shop, and helpful (never salesy) presence in owner Facebook groups. Free audits for the first 3 shops buy case studies; implementation retainers are the business.
