## 4. Monday morning, minute by minute

**Best windows:** 7:00-8:30am (owner is in the shop before dispatch) and 4:30-6:30pm (owner is back in the truck and will pick up a cell). 1:00-3:00pm works for the office line. Avoid 8:30-10:00am; that is dispatch chaos. At 2-5 trucks the "office line" is usually the owner's cell or the spouse who does the books, so treat whoever answers as a decision maker. Owners who cannot answer still read texts, which is why every call gets a text within five minutes.

**Block:** 7:30-8:30 Tier A calls (10-12 dials). 8:30-9:00 log outcomes, send the follow-up texts and emails. 1:00-2:30 second block (Tier A leftovers + Tier B). 2:30-3:00 log, text, schedule call-backs. Target for the day: 25 dials, 5 conversations with an owner or GM, 2 Leak Checks booked.

**Before you dial:** read the "Opening Line" and "Observable Leaks" cells for that row. You are calling with one specific fact about their business. That is the whole difference between you and the five "never miss a call again" robocalls they got last week.

## 5. The Leak Check call itself (how the free call turns into $2,500)

1. **Intake (5 min):** trucks, techs, office staff, software they pay for, who answers the phone after 5pm, how many open estimates right now, review count vs the competitor two miles away, hours a week the owner spends on paperwork, what they pay the agency.
2. **Mirror it back (10 min):** "Here's what I heard" with a dollar figure next to each leak (use the math in section 2). Owners buy when they hear their own numbers.
3. **The ask (5 min):** "If you want, the next step is the three-week audit: I interview your office, your techs and whoever does the books, I map where the work gets stuck, and you get a plan with the three fixes ranked by payback and a five-year target you can run the business on. $2,500, half to start, and it comes off any fix you hire me for. Want me to send the one-page agreement?"
4. **Same day:** send the agreement (use the ICA template Antonisha Baker finalized in June) and the NDA. Deposit invoice via Stripe. Book the first interview before you hang up.

What the audit deliverable looks like (you already built this): the role-based interview guides, the 12-domain / 36-check question bank, the weighted scorecard, the goal-anchored presentation ("from 5 jobs a day to 7"), and the 5-year plan format from FMBS.

## 7. Three things that make this land faster

1. **Lead with FMBS, every time.** It is a local, named, three-company merger with a result. Nobody else cold-calling HVAC owners in Broward has that. Get Cornel or Maurice to say two sentences you can quote by Wednesday.
2. **Fix the thing you can see.** Missing license number on the website is a Florida statute 489.119(5)(b) issue. No booking button, no reviews on the site, two different phone numbers. The owner can verify every claim in 30 seconds, so your credibility is instant.
3. **Say "office side", not "AI".** Nick at Bee Cool said he will "not ever" let AI answer phones, and most owners agree. AI drafting with a human hard-stop is a detail for the audit, not the headline. The headline is the five-year numbers in two.
