## 2. Top 5 objections to consultants and evidence-based responses

1. **"That's $15K+ a year I don't have."** (Nexstar ~$15K/yr; groups $1,200-4,800/mo.) Response: price against one unbilled change order or one 2-3 hour parts run per truck per week; the Mike Holt members called it "well worth it" only because they could see the number it moved.
2. **"Last guy took my money and I got nothing."** (SEO $15K, zero sales; 1 real lead in 20.) Response: no long contract, a 30-day scoped deliverable, and refuse to touch marketing spend until the operations leak is fixed.
3. **"I don't have time to put 100% into it."** Response: this is why coaching groups fail small shops; offer done-with-you work on their existing software instead of homework.
4. **"We're too small for this."** ("It's to much for our small company.") Response: the 2-15 truck shop misses 55-65% of calls; fixing intake and billing is sized for them, not for a 50-truck shop.
5. **"We already pay for software that should do this."** ("stuck paying for a database we will never use.") Response: lead with configuring what they already own (Housecall Pro / ServiceTitan / Jobber + QuickBooks) so the first win costs no new subscription.

## 3. Best times and ways to reach owners (partly inference, marked)

- **Who answers:** at 2-5 trucks the "office line" is usually the owner's cell or the spouse-bookkeeper; a dedicated receptionist appears around 6-15 employees (CallJolt).
- **When they can't talk:** during a heat wave or cold snap they are "overwhelmed with calls" (CBS Boston) and on roofs; 55-75% of inbound calls go unanswered. **[inference]** Avoid the first hot week of summer and the first freeze.
- **When they can:** **[inference]** 7:00-8:00 am before dispatch, or 4:30-6:30 pm when the owner is back in the truck; shoulder season (Sept-Oct, Mar-Apr) is the planning window.
- **Channel:** owners who cannot answer still read texts; a short text naming the specific leak ("unbilled extras", "QuickBooks duplicates") then a scheduled callback outperforms a cold voice call. **[inference]**
