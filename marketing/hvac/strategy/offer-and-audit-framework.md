## 2. The audit framework — "The 5 Leaks"

This is both your delivery checklist AND your content engine. Every leak generates a dozen pieces of content.

### Leak 1 — Missed & after-hours calls
- HVAC owners miss roughly a quarter or more of inbound calls (job sites, after hours, weekends). An unanswered emergency call goes straight to the next company on Google.
- **Audit questions:** What happens to a call at 7pm Friday? Who answers when techs are on roofs? Call log review: rings vs. answers vs. booked.
- **Dollar math for content:** avg residential ticket $450–$600, replacement lead worth $8K–$15K. 10 missed calls/week × 30% would-have-booked × $500 = **$6,500/mo leak**.
- **AI fix:** AI receptionist / missed-call text-back (answers 24/7, books directly onto the schedule).

### Leak 2 — Unsold estimates (no follow-up)
- Most shops send a quote and never follow up. Close rates on followed-up estimates roughly double.
- **Audit questions:** How many open estimates right now? Who follows up, when, how many times? What's in the "quoted, never heard back" pile from last 90 days?
- **AI fix:** automated estimate follow-up sequence (text + email, day 1/3/7/14), AI-drafted personalized nudges.

### Leak 3 — Reviews & reputation
- Owners with 40 reviews lose the Google Map Pack to competitors with 400. Techs "forget" to ask.
- **Audit questions:** Review count vs. top 3 local competitors, review velocity last 90 days, response rate to reviews.
- **AI fix:** automated post-job review requests, AI-drafted review responses, negative-review interception.

### Leak 4 — Office admin drowning
- The owner (or their spouse) spends nights on invoicing, dispatch juggling, permit paperwork, payroll prep.
- **Audit questions:** Hours/week on paperwork? What still lives on paper or in texts? How many times is the same job typed into different systems?
- **AI fix:** AI dispatch summaries, auto-invoicing from job notes, document/permit drafting, quote generation from photos.

### Leak 5 — Feast-or-famine marketing
- Owners buy leads (Angi/Thumbtack at $50–$150 each, shared with 4 competitors) instead of owning a database. Slow season = panic.
- **Audit questions:** Cost per booked job by source? Size of customer list? Any maintenance-agreement program? What goes out to past customers?
- **AI fix:** AI-written seasonal campaigns to the existing customer list, maintenance-plan enrollment sequences, content generation for their own social.

### Audit delivery mechanics (keep it light)
1. 20-minute intake call or form (the 5 Leaks questions above).
2. You + AI analyze: their Google profile, reviews, website, response time (mystery-call them after hours — with permission), and their numbers.
3. Produce the Leak Report + Priority Plan (template once, reuse forever).
4. 30-minute walkthrough. End with: "Want this fixed? Here's what that looks like."

## 3. Why owners will trust a stranger on the internet

The audience is skeptical of "AI bros." Every piece of content must obey these rules:

1. **Talk trade, not tech.** Never "leverage AI solutions." Always "your phone rings at 9pm and nobody picks up."
2. **Dollars, not features.** Every claim gets a number: calls, tickets, close rates, dollars per month.
3. **Show, don't tell.** Screen recordings of an AI answering a call, a follow-up text going out, a review reply being drafted — beat any talking-head claim.
4. **Respect the trade.** They built these businesses sweating in attics. The tone is "you're great at HVAC, the phone system is robbing you" — never "you're doing it wrong."
5. **No hype.** No "AI will replace everything." One specific problem, one specific fix, one number, per piece.
