# HVAC Content Strategy — ClearFrequency AI Profit Audit

13 weeks · Mon Sep 14 – Fri Dec 11, 2026 · 5 pieces/week · 65 pieces total
Goal: build trust with HVAC owners (2–10 trucks) and generate audit bookings — first client within 30 days.

## Platform playbook (research-driven)

| Platform | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook Reels + feed** | PRIMARY | Owners are 40–60; 80% of US adults 30–49 use FB; owner groups live here. Low Reels competition = high avg views. |
| **Instagram Reels** | PRIMARY (same upload) | Widest cross-demo short-form reach, best at driving action. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Secondary — trust layer | Shorts funnel into longer demos/case studies later. |
| **TikTok** | Repurpose only | Skews 18–34 techs, not owners. Post it, don't optimize for it. |
| **FB Groups (as yourself)** | Highest-intent channel | Value-first comments/posts in owner groups — never pitch in-group. Detailed in 30-day plan. |

**Workflow: make once → post everywhere.** Same 9:16 vertical video to all four. Captions adjusted per platform only if time allows.

## The 5 content pillars (= the 5 Leaks + trust)

1. **Missed calls / after-hours** (biggest wedge — 27% missed, $500–$1,200 each, 85% never call back)
2. **Unsold estimates / follow-up** (biggest hidden money — 70% of quotes walk)
3. **Reviews, marketing & seasonality** (91% check reviews; 40% cite cash flow as #1 problem)
4. **Admin/office overload** (owner or spouse doing invoices at 9pm)
5. **Trust & anti-hype** (myth-busting, demos, honest-broker positioning, audit walkthroughs)

## Monthly arcs

- **Month 1 (W1–4): Name the leaks.** Problem awareness with dollar math. Soft CTA (follow, comment "AUDIT").
- **Month 2 (W5–8): Show, don't tell.** Demos, fixes, anti-hype, first free-audit offer to South Florida shops.
- **Month 3 (W9–13): Authority & conversion.** Objection handling, audit walkthroughs, year-end/2027 urgency, direct CTAs.

## Voice rules (from research — non-negotiable)

- Trade language, used correctly: trucks, CSR, no-cool calls, tune-ups, shoulder season, maintenance agreements, "the big guys."
- Every claim has a number. Ranges, not gospel ("$500–$1,200 per missed call").
- No hype, no "AI will change everything," no agency-speak. You are the honest broker who's on their side against tool-sellers and lead-sellers.
- Respect: "you're great at HVAC; your phone system is robbing you" — never "you're doing it wrong."
- POV/skit formats are allowed and encouraged — contractor life comedy earns trust.

## Faceless video production kit — file template

Every video piece file (`marketing/hvac/content/week-NN/VNN-slug.md`) contains:

```
# VNN — [Title]
**Post date:** [date] · **Pillar:** [pillar] · **Length:** 30–45s

## Hook (0–2s, spoken + on-screen)
## Full voiceover script (word-for-word, ~90–120 words)
## Shot-by-shot plan
  (table: timestamp | visual | b-roll search terms for CapCut/Pexels stock | on-screen text)
## CapCut assembly notes (voice: built-in AI voice, style suggestion; music mood; caption style)
## Post caption (FB/IG version)
## Hashtags (10–15, mixed reach)
## CTA
```

**Assembly workflow (10–15 min/video):** CapCut mobile/desktop → paste script into Text-to-Speech (pick one AI voice and NEVER change it — voice consistency = brand) → drop in stock b-roll per shot list (CapCut library or pexels.com, search terms provided) → auto-captions on, bold style, keywords highlighted → on-screen text per plan → trending low-key music at 10% volume → export 1080×1920.

## Carousel template (`CNN-slug.md`)
Title, post date, slide-by-slide (headline + body + visual note per slide), caption, hashtags. Design in Canva: bold text on high-contrast background, first slide = pure hook.

## Text post template (`TNN-slug.md`)
Full post text (FB-native length, line breaks for scannability), first line = hook, ends with question or soft CTA.

## Metrics that matter (check weekly, 15 min)
- Follows from owner-looking accounts; comments containing "AUDIT"; DMs; profile clicks.
- Ignore raw views on TikTok (tech audience inflates/deflates meaninglessly).
- Double down on any format that gets owner comments; kill what gets silence 3 weeks running.
