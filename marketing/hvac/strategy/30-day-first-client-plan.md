# 30-Day First-Client Plan (Sep 14 – Oct 13, 2026)

**Goal:** 1 paying-path client in 30 days — meaning at minimum a completed audit with a shop that fits the implementation profile, ideally a signed implementation deal.
**Time budget:** ~2 hrs/day (content posting is ~20 min/day since videos are batch-assembled Sundays).
**Reality check:** content alone rarely converts strangers in 30 days. Content builds trust; **direct outreach + Facebook groups get the first client.** The content makes the outreach land ("I checked her page — she actually knows our business").

---

## The math (so you know what "enough activity" looks like)

100 South Florida shops on a hit list → ~25 personalized outreaches/week → 10–15% reply to genuinely personalized messages → 8–12 conversations → 3–4 booked audits → **1–2 implementation clients.** Free audits remove all buying friction; the sale happens on the walkthrough call.

---

## Week 0 — Setup (do before Sep 14, ~4 hours total)

1. **Profiles**: FB business page + IG + TikTok + YouTube, same name/handle everywhere (e.g., "Ann Kelly | AI for HVAC"). Bio formula: *"I find the $3K–$10K/mo your HVAC business is leaking — and show you how to get it back. Free audit → link."* Banner + profile photo consistent.
2. **Booking link**: Calendly event "AI Profit Audit — Intake Call (20 min)" with buffer, linked in every bio.
3. **Audit assets**: Leak Report template (1-page scorecard from `offer-and-audit-framework.md`), 12-question intake form (Google Form). Do this once; reuse forever.
4. **Hit list (100 shops)**: Google Maps "AC repair" / "HVAC" in Fort Lauderdale, Pompano, Hollywood, Boca, West Palm, Miami Gardens. Qualify: 3.5–4.7 stars, 15–200 reviews, has a website, looks like 2–10 trucks (photos show multiple vans). Spreadsheet: name, owner name (check website/FB "About"), phone, review count, top competitor's review count, one observable leak.
5. **Join 5 FB groups** (as yourself, personal profile): HVAC Business Owners & Contractors · Blue Collar Millionaire · Service Business Growth (warriorglobal) · HVAC Masters of The Hustle · one local: "South Florida Contractors" / Broward business networking groups. **Say nothing salesy for 2 weeks. Only answer questions helpfully.**

## Week 1 (Sep 14–20) — Post, listen, build evidence

- Content W1 goes live daily (already scheduled). Reply to every comment within an hour when possible.
- **Evidence pass on 25 hit-list shops (30 min/day)**: for each shop record ONE verifiable leak — called at 6:30pm and got voicemail; last Google review reply was 8 months ago; competitor 2 miles away has 6× their reviews; website has no booking link. This is your outreach ammo. (One brief after-hours call to hear whether a human/service answers is fine; don't waste their time or fake being a customer beyond that.)
- **FB groups**: 2 helpful comments/day. When someone complains about missed calls, office chaos, dead estimates — give a complete, generous answer with zero pitch. Owners check profiles of helpful people; your content does the selling.
- Ask 3 people who already know you (ClearFrequency network, chamber, CareerSource contacts) : "Who do you know that owns an AC company?" Warm intros beat everything.

## Week 2 (Sep 21–27) — Outreach wave 1 (25 shops)

Channel order: **FB page DM or personal FB of owner → email from website → text to office line** (calls only if they engage). 5/day.

**DM/email script (personalize the bracketed parts, keep it this short):**
> Hi [Name] — I called [Company] Tuesday at 6:40pm about an AC issue and got voicemail. About 85% of homeowners who hit voicemail just call the next company on Google — at your ticket sizes that's real money walking every week.
> I do free AI Profit Audits for a handful of South Florida HVAC shops — 30 minutes, I show you exactly where calls, estimates and follow-ups are leaking and what it'd cost to fix. No software pitch, I don't sell a tool. Worth a look? [Calendly link]

- Log every send + response in the spreadsheet. Follow-up cadence: bump at day 3 ("floating this up — happy to just send you the 2 things I noticed, no call needed"), day 8 (send one finding for free, ask nothing).
- **The "give it away" variant** (highest conversion): for the 5 best-fit shops, don't ask for a call — just send the mini-finding: "Your competitor [X] has 412 reviews to your 67 and replies to all of them — that's most of why they outrank you on Maps. Two more things like this if you want them." Generosity converts skeptics.

## Week 3 (Sep 28 – Oct 4) — Wave 2 + first audits

- Outreach wave 2: next 25 shops, same playbook, refined with what got replies.
- Run every booked audit within 48 hours of the intake call. Deliver the Leak Report visually (share screen), always ending with: "If you want, I can set the top fix up for you — here's what that costs and what it returns."
- **FB groups**: you've earned the right to post once now. Value post, no link: "I've been auditing South FL HVAC shops this month. Same 3 leaks every time: [breakdown with numbers]. Happy to answer questions." If group rules allow, mention free audits only when asked or in comments.
- Ask every audit — client or not — for one referral: "Which owner do you know that's drowning in missed calls?"

## Week 4 (Oct 5–13) — Convert

- T08 content offer ("3 free audits for South FL shops") actually drops Nov 3 in the calendar — **pull it forward**: post the offer as a text post on your page and in any group that allows it this week, since outreach has warmed the pond.
- Follow up every open conversation. Objection answers: *"Too busy"* → "That's the leak. 20 minutes, I do all the work." *"Already have an answering service"* → "Great — the audit checks what they're booking vs. dropping. Most owners are surprised."
- Close implementation with the first audit that shows a clear missed-call or estimate-follow-up leak: **$1,500–$2,500 setup + $500–$750/mo** (intro pricing for client #1, in exchange for a testimonial + numbers you can cite). One recovered install (~$10K ticket) pays their year.
- **Podcast seed (15 min, pays off in months 2–3):** pitch Service Business Mastery + Owned and Operated as a guest: "what I found auditing South Florida HVAC shops — the 5 leaks pattern."

## Scoreboard (check Friday, 10 min)

Outreaches sent · replies · audits booked · audits delivered · proposals out · **weekly target: 25 / 3+ / 1+ from week 2 on.** If replies are under 10% after 25 sends, the personalization is too thin — go deeper on the observable leak, not louder on the pitch.

**What NOT to do:** don't buy ads (you said no spend — good, you don't need them yet); don't pitch inside groups before week 3; don't build a website funnel first (Calendly + FB page is enough); don't wait for content to "take off" before outreach.
